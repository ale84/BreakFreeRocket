Break Free Tracker Privacy Policy

This policy covers Break Free Tracker, developed by Alessio Orlando. The section on RevenueCat applies from version 2.0.

Your data in the app

Your profile name, counters, goals, relapse history and notes are stored on your device. The app does not send this content to the developer or to RevenueCat. If you export a backup or share a counter, you choose where that copy goes. A copy you save or share can remain there until you delete it.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes App Store purchases and payments. Break Free uses RevenueCat in version 2.0 to verify and restore purchases, check subscription status, unlock Premium and understand how purchase offers perform.

RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous app identifier generated by its SDK, purchase and subscription information such as product and transaction identifiers, and interactions with the Premium screen, such as when an offer is shown or closed. The app does not give RevenueCat your profile name, email address or payment card details. We do not use this information for advertising or to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

RevenueCat processes this information on our behalf. It may process data in the United States. Its data processing agreement includes standard contractual clauses where applicable. You can contact us for information about these safeguards. See the RevenueCat Data Processing Addendum and Apple’s App Store privacy notice.

Support and how we use data

If you email support, we receive your email address and what you choose to send. The message prepared in the app includes the app and operating system versions. We use this information to respond and resolve the issue.

Where the GDPR applies, we process purchase data as needed to provide and restore what you bought. We use limited purchase and Premium screen data for our legitimate interest in understanding and improving our offers, and support data to respond to your request.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local data stays on your device until you delete it from the app or remove the app. You manage exported copies and any device backups separately. We retain purchase records for as long as needed to verify or restore access, handle disputes or meet legal obligations. We retain Premium interaction data for as long as needed to assess and improve our offers. We retain support messages for as long as needed to handle your request and related follow-up.

For privacy questions or to request access, correction or deletion of data we process, email breakfreeiosapp@gmail.com. Where applicable, you can also request portability or restriction, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and complain to your data protection authority. You can manage or cancel App Store subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.
