Break Free Tracker

Count the days since you left a habit behind.

Create a counter for each habit you want to quit and see how far you’ve come, one day at a time.

Free to use, with no ads

  • Start multiple counters and set a first goal for each one, or count days with no end date.
  • Log relapses and add notes to remember what happened.
  • See your longest streak, completed goals and archived counters in Results.
  • Check your progress with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and turn on milestone notifications.

Break Free Premium

The optional subscription lets you look back at starts, relapses and milestones on the calendar, explore advanced statistics, and add more goals to the same counter or edit an active goal. You can also choose counter colours and animations to celebrate reaching a goal.
