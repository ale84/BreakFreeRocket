What’s new in Break Free 2.0

Your counters, relapses and notes are already in the app. Here’s where to find them and how to get started with the new features.

Set a goal

Open a counter and tap Add in the Goal section. Pick the duration you want to reach and follow your progress in the counter details.

Find your progress in Results

The Results tab brings together your statistics and archive. With the Premium calendar, you can select a day to see what happened and which counters were active.

Manage your passcode and backups in Profile

Open Profile to set a passcode, export or import your data, and restore purchases.
